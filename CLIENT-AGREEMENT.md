### 1. What I offer

I'm Ian. I've been through mental health difficulty, physical health
difficulty, and serious money trouble, usually all at once. What I offer is:

- Someone to talk to who already understands the shape of it
- Company while you do the hard practical parts — opening mail, making calls,
  filling in forms, booking appointments
- Help finding real professionals, and staying on the line while you contact
  them
- Someone who follows up when you go quiet

### 2. What this is not

**I am not a therapist, counsellor, psychologist, social worker, doctor, or
financial adviser, and this is not treatment.**

- Not therapy, counselling, or any clinical care
- Not medical advice — never delay or stop treatment because of something I say
- Not financial, debt, or legal advice. I'll tell you hardship programs exist
  and sit with you while you call. I won't negotiate for you or advise on
  specific debts
- **Not an emergency service**

If what you need is a therapist, I'll help you find one and stay with you while
you book it. That's a good outcome, not me passing you off.

### 3. If you're in crisis

I don't monitor messages around the clock. In an emergency:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **988** | Suicide & Crisis Lifeline — call or text, 24/7 |
| **741741** | Crisis Text Line — text HOME |
| **1-800-952-2335** | Santa Cruz County Behavioral Health crisis line, 24/7 |
| **911** | Immediate danger |

### 4. What I keep private, and the one limit

What you tell me stays with me. I don't discuss you with anyone, I never
screenshot messages, and I won't share your story publicly — even anonymised —
without asking you in writing first and asking again before publishing.

**The one limit, and I'm telling you now so it's never a surprise:**

> If I genuinely believe you or someone else is in immediate danger of dying,
> I will contact emergency services or a crisis line and give them what they
> need to help.

I will not promise you secrecy I can't keep. If you want to talk to someone
with no such limit, that is exactly what 988 is for, and I will never think
less of you for using it.

I'm also not a healthcare provider, so the legal confidentiality that covers a
doctor or therapist does **not** apply here. I'll treat what you tell me as
carefully as I know how, but you should know the difference.

### 5. Hours, and what to expect

- I answer between ______ and ______, ____________________
- I usually reply within a day
- Outside those hours, 988 covers what I can't

I may be slower sometimes. I may need to pause. I'll tell you if so rather
than going quiet on you.

### 6. My capacity

I work with a small number of people at a time, on purpose, so that nobody
gets a half-present version of me. If I'm full I'll say so and offer a
waitlist or a referral rather than stretching myself thin.

### 7. Cost

**Free.** First Light calls and the Warm Line cost nothing and always will.

If we ever agree on paid work — ongoing one-to-one sessions, a workshop — we'll
agree the price in writing first. You will never be upsold during a hard
conversation. ☐ *Tick if paid terms are attached separately.*

### 8. Ending it

You can stop at any time, without explanation, and you don't owe me a goodbye
message.

I may also end or decline support — if what you need is beyond peer support,
or if the relationship stops being safe or useful for either of us. If that
happens I'll say so plainly and help you find somewhere better.

### 9. Recording

Neither of us records calls without the other agreeing in writing first.
California requires everyone's consent.

### 10. Your decisions stay yours

Nothing I say obliges you to do anything. Decisions about your health, money,
and life remain entirely yours, and so does responsibility for them.

### 11. Adults only

You confirm you are 18 or over.

### 12. Law

This agreement is governed by California law, in Santa Cruz County.
