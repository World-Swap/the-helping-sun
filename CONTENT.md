# Content & Social

## Platform choice

Pick **two**, not five. Everything else is a placeholder handle you claim and
leave idle.

1. **Instagram** — where friends, family and local Santa Cruz people already
   are. Reels + carousels. This is home base.
2. **Golden Hour (the newsletter)** — the owned asset. Platforms change their
   algorithm and your reach disappears; an email list is yours. Beehiiv or
   Ghost, free tier.

Add **YouTube Shorts or TikTok** only once you're posting consistently for a
month without strain.

**Reddit is where people actually ask for help** (r/depression, r/ADHD,
r/povertyfinance, r/Santa_Cruz). Self-promotion gets you banned instantly.
Participate as a human being, with no links, because it's the right thing to
do. Trust compounds. Never post the brand there.

## The five content pillars

### 1. The Hard Stuff
First-person, specific, unresolved. The whole value is in the detail nobody
else will name.

> Not: "Depression makes daily tasks difficult."
> But: "There were four unopened envelopes on my counter for eleven weeks. I
> knew one of them was from the DMV. I knew it was getting worse. I still
> couldn't touch them."

### 2. One Small Thing
A single doable action per post. No lists of ten.
*Open the blinds. Ten minutes of light before noon. Drink the water before the
coffee. Say the word "hardship" on the phone.*

### 3. The Scripts ⭐
**Make this your signature.** What to literally say, word for word:

- what to say to a creditor to get a hardship plan
- what to say to a doctor when you've been avoiding it for two years
- what to say to a friend you ghosted for six months
- what to say to your boss when you can't explain why
- what to say when someone tells you they want to die

Nobody does this well, it's endlessly shareable, and it's useful the moment
it's read. Carousels on Instagram; one script per post.

### 4. The Sunrise Log
A photo, the time, one line. On a bad day this is the whole post, and that's
precisely the message. Daily if you can — the consistency *is* the content.

### 5. Ask The Sun
Anonymous questions in, honest answers out. Starts slow. Eventually becomes the
thing people come for. Use an anonymous form so nobody has to be brave twice.

## Format rules

- Face to camera, no music, no jump cuts. Polish reads as a sales pitch here;
  plainness reads as true.
- Captions on everything — most people watch muted.
- Never post from inside a bad day. Draft it, hold it, publish when you're on
  the other side. The one exception is a short, calm "today is hard, I'm okay,
  back tomorrow", which is honest. Raw distress is not content; it is you
  needing support, and you deserve the real thing.
- Every post about crisis carries 988 in the caption.
- Never use someone's story without written permission. See `SAFETY-AND-LEGAL.md` §6.

## First 30 days

Three posts a week. That's it. Consistency beats volume, and the schedule has
to survive your worst week, not your best one.

| Week | Posts |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Who I am and why (face to camera, 60s) · Sunrise Log · Script: *what to say to a creditor* |
| 2 | The Hard Stuff: the unopened mail · One Small Thing: light before noon · Sunrise Log |
| 3 | Script: *telling a doctor you've been avoiding it* · The Hard Stuff: looking fine · Sunrise Log |
| 4 | What The Helping Sun is and isn't (the honest one) · Script: *what to say when someone says they want to die* · Sunrise Log + newsletter launch |

## The launch post

Don't announce a business. Tell the truth and mention that you've made
somewhere for it to go. Something close to:

> I spent most of my life depressed, broke, and too embarrassed to tell anyone.
> I got through it without anyone reaching back, and I don't recommend it.
>
> I've started something called The Helping Sun. It isn't therapy and I'm not a
> therapist. It's me, answering, for people who are carrying something heavy and
> can't ask for help nicely.
>
> If that's you, the link is in my bio. If it's someone you love, send it to
> them. You don't have to say anything else.

Post it and then put the phone down for two hours. Genuinely.

## A caution about going viral

Posts like these occasionally reach a very large number of people at once, and
a share of them will reply needing real help. You cannot answer two thousand
messages, and trying will take you apart.

Decide the response *now*: a pinned comment with 988, 741741 and 211, an
auto-reply naming your real capacity, and no guilt about either. Being honest
about your limits is part of the service, not a failure of it.
