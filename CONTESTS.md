## Before you enter anything: read the rights clause

This matters more for you than for most entrants, because **you sell prints
of these photographs**. A contest that takes broad rights can undercut the
thing paying for The Helping Sun.

**Walk away from:**

- **Perpetual** or **irrevocable** licences — forever, and you cannot cancel
- **Sublicensable** or **transferable** — they can hand your work to anyone
- "Unrestricted licence", "for any purpose" — no limit on commercial use
- Any **transfer of copyright**, even if only for winners
- **Indemnification** clauses that put their legal costs on you
- Terms that bind **all entrants**, not just winners

**Look for:**

- "Copyright remains with the photographer"
- A **non-exclusive** licence
- A **stated duration** — a year, or the promotion period, not forever
- A **stated purpose** — promoting the contest, not general commercial use
- Credit to you by name, every time

If the terms are unclear, email and ask. An organiser who will not answer
plainly has told you something.

Sony's terms are the benchmark to compare against; they are a real
organisation with a reputation to protect, which is part of why their
competition is the first on this list.

## Entered

### Sony World Photography Awards — both competitions

**Single Image** — three photographs, Landscape category. Closes 5 January
2027, 16:00 GMT. Entry IDs 7466225, 7466229, 7466233 — all three confirmed
**Submitted**, one image each.

**Series** — ten photographs, Landscape category, one sunset on 5 November
2026 in sequence. Entry ID 7466265, confirmed **Submitted**, 10 images.
Closes 12 January 2027 (a week after Single Image — two separate deadlines,
so do not assume one date covers both).

The archive holds **14 photographs from that evening, 4:20pm to 5:25pm** — 64
minutes. Entering ten of them as a progression is the right use of a Series
competition: a series is judged as one work, and judges are looking for a
reason the pictures belong together. "One evening, start to finish" is that
reason, and it is self-evident without a word of explanation.

Nothing more to do on either until the results. Both are free, so there is no
payment to confirm and no renewal to miss.

**What to watch:** Sony notify shortlists by email, and the address they have
is whichever one you entered with. If that is not the Zoho address you check,
set a forward now rather than finding out in March.
