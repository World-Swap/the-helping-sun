One block per image, in upload order. Copy each field into the
**Upload New Image** form.

Three fields are identical every time. Set them once under
**Edit Default Settings** on the upload page if it lets you:

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Artist Name** | Ian Sun |
| **Medium** | Photography |
| **Artwork Category** | Photograph |

**29 names are written** — those photographs were looked at
individually and the description says what is actually in the frame.

**69 names are generated** and marked ★. They come from
what can be measured in the file: the dominant colour of the sky, how
bright it is, the time of day and the month. Nothing about place or
subject is claimed, because that cannot be measured. **Treat them as
starting points and rename as you upload** — you are looking at the
photograph, I am looking at its pixels.

All 96 names are unique, and keywords are within the 500-character limit.
