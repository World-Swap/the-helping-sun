## 1. Structure — check the LLC before you use it

You have **SMG Payments LLC**, a California LLC from a business you started
and stopped. It has never traded. That is not the clean starting point it
sounds like, and the first job is finding out what state it's actually in.

### Step one, today: look it up. It's free and takes two minutes.

1. **California Secretary of State** — `bizfileOnline.sos.ca.gov`, search the
   name. You're looking at **Status**. "Active" is fine. **"Suspended – FTB"**
   or "FTB Forfeited" is the problem case.
2. **Franchise Tax Board** — log in at `ftb.ca.gov` (or call) and check the
   balance on the LLC.

### Why this matters more than it looks

California charges **$800 a year in minimum franchise tax for every LLC on the
Secretary of State's records — trading or not, earning or not.** Dormancy
changes nothing. It is owed for any year the LLC exists on 1 January, and it
does not prorate.

So if the LLC has been sitting there unused for a few years, it has very
likely been quietly accruing $800 a year, plus penalties and interest. On top
of that the biennial **Statement of Information** (Form LLC-12, $20) carries a
**$250 penalty** when late.

If unpaid for more than a year past the tax year, the FTB suspends the LLC.
A suspended LLC **cannot legally do business, cannot enforce its contracts,
and cannot even be cancelled** until it's revived with Form FTB 3557 and the
arrears are paid.

**This liability exists whether or not you ever use it for The Helping Sun.**
It doesn't go away by being ignored, and it grows every January.

### Then choose

| | When it fits | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A. Use it** | Active and current | File a DBA for "The Helping Sun" under the LLC with the Santa Cruz County Clerk (~$50–60 + four weeks' publication). You're paying the $800 anyway, so the entity is effectively free. |
| **B. Close it, run simple** | Suspended, or you don't want a $800/year bill for a free service | Revive it, cancel it (Form LLC-4/7), and run The Helping Sun as a **sole proprietor with a DBA**. No franchise tax at all. Perfectly adequate while everything is free. |
| **C. Close it, open clean** | You want the liability shield and a name that fits | Cancel SMG Payments, form **The Helping Sun LLC**. New $800/year, but no DBA needed and the name matches. |

**My read: B, unless the LLC is already clean and current.** You're pre-revenue
and everything you offer is free. $800 a year for a liability shield around a
service that takes no money is a bad trade, and insurance is what actually
protects you here. Revisit the LLC when money starts coming in.

If it turns out to be Active and paid up, A is the obvious answer — don't
throw away something you've already paid for.

### A note on the name

"SMG Payments" reads as a financial services company. You'll be talking to
people about debt and hardship, and a payments-branded entity behind that is
at best confusing and at worst looks like something it isn't. A DBA solves it
on paper, but it's one more reason not to force this entity into the role.

### Talk to whoever did the LLC

If an accountant or filing service set it up, ask them to pull the status and
tell you the exact arrears before you decide. An hour of a CPA's time here is
cheap next to a surprise multi-year franchise tax bill.

### Until it's settled

The privacy policy, terms and client agreement name **Ian Sun** as the
operator, which is accurate today — the LLC has never traded and no DBA links
it to The Helping Sun. Add the entity line to all three once you've chosen and
filed. Don't name a suspended LLC as the operator of a live service.

⚠️ **Still true:** don't solicit public donations without charitable
registration or a fiscal sponsor.

## 2. The first 90 days

- [ ] Decide: use it, close it, or replace it (§1)

- [x] Site deployed to `thehelpingsun.com` — live, auto-deploys on push
- [ ] Crisis protocol **printed** (county number is in it: 1-800-952-2335)
- [ ] Insurance quotes requested
- [x] Instagram `@the_helping_sun` and Facebook `thehelpingsun` claimed
- [ ] Claim the same name on TikTok, YouTube and Threads (park them, don't post)
- [ ] **Your own support booked** — therapist or peer group
- [ ] Ask one person to be your co-pilot

### Days 15–30 — Ten quiet conversations

**Do not launch publicly yet.** Instead: ten free *First Light* calls with
friends, family, and people they refer. No posts, no logo reveal.

This is market research wearing kind clothes. After each call write down:
*What did they actually need? What did they ask for? What did they refuse?*

You will discover the real business here, and it will not be what you expected.
Most people in this category find the demand is for **practical navigation**
(forms, calls, appointments) far more than for conversation.

**The one metric that matters: people who reached out.** Not followers.

### Days 31–60 — Start talking

- Newsletter live (**Golden Hour**, weekly) on Beehiiv or Ghost
- **One** social platform, properly — not five, badly
- Publish 3×/week (see `CONTENT.md`)
- Order **the card** (see below)

### Days 61–90 — First offers

- Open **Sunrise Circle** with six people
- First paid 1:1 slots
- First B2B conversation (see §3)
- Review: is the capacity cap holding? Are you okay?

## 3. How this makes money

Ordered by how soon each is realistic. The free tier is permanent and is the
whole point — it is also the top of the funnel, and those two facts aren't in
conflict as long as you never pressure anyone upward.

| | Offer | Price | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | First Light, Warm Line, newsletter | $0 | Permanent. Never gated. |
| 1 | **Sunrise Circle** membership | $15–25/mo | Six people, weekly hour. Low effort, recurring, and the most *useful* thing on the list. |
| 2 | **1:1 accountability** | $75–150/session, or $250–400/month | Non-clinical. Needs insurance and a client agreement. |
| 3 | **Workshops** | $25–50/seat | "Money Shame", "Getting Through Winter", "How To Ask For Help". Record once, run repeatedly. |
| 4 | **Talks** | $500–2,500 | ⭐ The real money. Trade schools, unions, small employers, churches, recovery orgs — all have budgets for a speaker on mental health and financial stress, and your lived experience is exactly what gets booked. One talk a month beats 300 subscribers. |
| 5 | **Prints of the sunrise photos** | $25–60 | You already have the inventory. A stated % to the Helping Hand fund. Low volume, high brand value. |
| 6 | Grants / sponsorship | — | Only after nonprofit or fiscal sponsorship. |

**Don't:** ad revenue (needs scale you won't have for years), affiliate
supplements or "mindset" products (destroys the trust that is your only asset),
anything requiring you to be available 24/7.

## 4. Two cheap ideas with outsized returns

**The Helping Sun card.** A physical business card: the name, the sun mark,
your number, and one line —

> *Text me when it's bad. 2 p.m. or 2 a.m.*

~$30 for 500. Hand them to friends, family, bartenders, barbers, trade-school
instructors, the guy at the hardware store. This solves the exact problem you
described: it reaches people who will never fill in a web form, and it gives
*other* people something to pass along when they don't know what to say.

**The Sunrise List.** A spreadsheet: who you're checking on, what's going on,
when you last spoke, when to reach out next. Unglamorous, and it *is* the
business. The product you're actually selling is that someone remembered.

## 5. What success looks like at 6 months

Not followers. Not revenue, yet.

- 25+ people have reached out
- 6 people in the Circle, showing up weekly
- One person who says you changed something — in writing, with permission
- You are still well. The cap held. You took time off.

If the last one isn't true, nothing else counts.
