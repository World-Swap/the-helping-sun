## About / Our Story
Paste this whole block.

```
WHO'S BEHIND THIS

I'm Ian Sun. I've spent most of my life on the other side of this page —
depressed, isolated, broke, and too embarrassed to say any of it out loud. I
continue to try and get through it without anyone reaching back. I don't
recommend that route.

The Helping Sun exists because I kept waiting for someone to notice, and
nobody did. Not because people are cruel — because I got very good at looking
fine. If that's you, I already know what you're doing, and you don't have to
explain it to me.

I'm not a therapist. I'm the person who answers. I'll sit with you while you
open the mail you've been avoiding, or make the call you can't make, or just
say the true thing out loud for the first time.

WHAT I OFFER

First Light — a free 30-minute call. No agenda, no form to fill out first, no
need to have it organised in your head.

The Warm Line — text check-ins on a schedule you set. Daily, weekly, or
only-when-it's-bad.

Sunrise Circle — a small weekly group. Opening early 2027.

A Helping Hand — practical help. A ride, a form filled out together, a phone
call you can't face making. Santa Cruz County first.

WHAT THIS IS NOT

Not therapy, counselling, or clinical treatment. Not medical advice. Not
financial, legal, or debt advice. Not a crisis line and not an emergency
service.

If what you need is a therapist, I'll help you find one and stay on the line
while you book the appointment.

IF YOU NEED HELP RIGHT NOW

988 — Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, call or text, 24/7
741741 — Crisis Text Line, text HOME
211 — Housing, food, utilities, local help
911 — Immediate danger

thehelpingsun.com
```

## Call-to-action button
Set it to **Learn More** → `https://thehelpingsun.com`

Not "Send Message". Message means Messenger, and Messenger means notifications
you will feel obliged to answer instantly at any hour. Route people to the
site's form, which you answer on your own schedule.

## Other fields
- **Email:** the real `hello@thehelpingsun.com` mailbox
- **Website:** `https://thehelpingsun.com`
- **Location:** Santa Cruz, CA. City only — never a street address
- **Hours:** set your real answering hours, e.g. 9 a.m.–9 p.m. Publishing
  hours is what makes the limit real rather than something you feel guilty
  about

# A note on comments and DMs

The day you post something honest, you will get replies. Some will be lovely
and some will be people in real trouble. Decide now:

- A pinned comment with 988 / 741741 / 211 on every heavy post
- An Instagram auto-reply stating when you actually answer
- Permission, from yourself, to not answer at 2 a.m.

Turn off comments on any post where you are not up to moderating them. That's
a legitimate use of the setting, not a failure.
