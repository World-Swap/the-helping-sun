One evening

The whole sunset, in order.

5 November 2026. I stood in one place from 4:20 to 5:25 pm and kept photographing while the light went. These are the frames in the order they happened — no rearranging, nothing skipped for being dull.

It is the clearest answer I have to what the photographs are for. Not one perfect picture. Just staying out there while it changed.
