The Archive

Santa Cruz County · 2026

Ian Sun · born in Hawaii, raised in Santa Cruz

It started with sunrises, on the mornings that were hardest to get up for. The photograph was never really the point — getting outside once and pressing a button was.

Then it stopped being only sunrises, and it stopped being only mornings. Mushrooms after the rain, storm surf on Christmas morning, sea stars at low water, the redwoods, a rainbow over a canopy turning red, a creek a long way from any ocean. Most of these were taken within a few miles of home on Monterey Bay; a few were not.

Prints of any of these pay for the running costs of The Helping Sun, so the support stays free for people who can't afford to ask. Choose a photograph, a size and a surface in the shop, and it's printed and posted to you directly.
