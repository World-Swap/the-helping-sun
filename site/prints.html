Prints

Santa Cruz County · 2026

I started photographing sunrises on the mornings that were hardest to get out of bed for. The photograph was never really the point — getting outside once and pressing a button was. These are what came back.

The Helping Sun is free, and I want to keep it that way. Nobody should have to weigh up whether they can afford to ask for help. But free isn't the same as costless — there's insurance, a phone, petrol for driving someone to an appointment, and a small fund for the practical things that actually move someone's week.

Prints are how those get paid for. Buy one and you're not donating to a charity — you're buying a photograph, and the margin keeps somebody's First Light call free.

What it covers

Liability insurance, the phone line, petrol for rides to appointments, and the Helping Hand fund for practical help.

Where it's printed

Bay Photo Lab in Scotts Valley — about fifteen minutes from where most of these were taken. Shipped straight to you.

What you get

Archival paper or metal, signed on the back with the date and place it was taken. No frame, so you can pick your own.
