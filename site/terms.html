What this service is

Peer support from someone with lived experience of mental health difficulty, physical health difficulty, and financial hardship. In practice that means:

  • Someone to talk to who already understands the shape of it
  • Company while you do hard administrative things — opening mail, making calls, filling in forms
  • Help finding real professionals, and staying with you while you contact them
  • Someone who follows up when you go quiet

What this service is not

This is not therapy or counselling. Ian Sun is not a licensed therapist, counsellor, psychologist, social worker, or medical professional, and nothing offered here is clinical treatment, diagnosis, or a substitute for care from someone qualified to give it.

This is not medical advice. Nothing said here should replace talking to a doctor. Never delay or stop medical treatment because of something you read or were told here.

This is not financial, debt, or legal advice. We will tell you that hardship programs exist and sit with you while you call. We will not negotiate on your behalf, advise on specific debts or investments, or do anything a licensed professional should do.

This is not an emergency service. We do not monitor messages around the clock. In a crisis, call or text 988, text HOME to 741741, or call 911.

You must be 18 or over

This service is for adults. If you're under 18, please speak to a school counsellor, a trusted adult, or contact 988 — they are set up to help you properly and we are not.

Availability and response times

Ian answers every message himself, usually within a day, during published hours. He is one person with his own limits and his own hard days. He may be slower than usual, may pause the service, and may need to stop entirely. None of these is a breach of anything — it's the honest shape of a service run by one human being.

If a reply matters urgently, please don't wait on us. Use 988.
